What is this dashboard?

This dashboard surfaces early warning signals, upcoming launches, and operational risks that could affect Frontline Escalated Support (TMS + SSA). It's designed to give senior leads a quick, daily read on what's happening across our channels and what's coming down the pipeline - without needing to check multiple tools.

The goal is a 2-minute scan that answers: Is anything on fire? What's coming? What needs attention today?

Data Sources

  • Slack (channels) Scans 4 core channels daily: #escalated-updates, #support-updates, #support-alerts, and #incident-announce. Reads all messages from the last 24 hours looking for escalations, complaints, volume spikes, unanswered questions, and decision threads.
  • Slack (keywords) Runs workspace-wide keyword searches across ALL Slack channels for: TMS, SSA, ExOD, Expert on Demand, Tier 2, Tier2, and Frontline Escalated. This catches relevant mentions in any channel - even ones not on the scan list.
  • Zendesk (Queue Health) Queries BigQuery ticket data for TMS and SSA Zendesk queues. Pulls active backlog, unassigned tickets, 24-hour volume trends, ticket age distribution, and on-hold counts. Only tracks "Technical Merchant Support" and "SSA Team" groups - Retail TMS and Plus TMS are excluded.
  • Birdwatcher Queries the Birdwatcher project context table in BigQuery for launches shipping in the next 14 days with medium or high support impact, training/doc gaps, or off-track status.
  • Calendar Pulls upcoming events tagged with launch, enablement, rollout, or readiness keywords from Google Calendar.
  • GitHub Checks the Escalated MF project board (#13430) in Shopify/psn for newly created or updated issues related to TMS/SSA operations.
  • Vault Searches for recent posts about TMS/SSA changes, Multifecta round-ups, new playbooks, or process updates from leadership.
  • IHC Flags communications and knowledge articles being shipped to the Internal Help Center that specialists may not have been briefed on yet.

How It Updates

A scheduled automation runs each weekday morning (EST). It scans all data sources, categorizes the signals, assigns a risk level, and publishes the results here. The dashboard always shows the most recent scan when you load the page.

Update Frequency

  • WeekdaysDaily scan at 7:30am EST
  • WeekendsNo automatic scan (manual runs available on request)

Data Freshness

Each scan covers the previous 24 hours of Slack activity and current-state data from Birdwatcher, GitHub, and Vault. The "Last Updated" timestamp in the footer shows when the most recent scan completed.

Manual Overrides

Katie McConnell (Ops MF) can trigger an ad-hoc scan at any time if something urgent comes up mid-day. The dashboard will update immediately after the scan completes.

Signal Categories

Each signal is classified into one of these categories based on what it describes:

ROUTING TOOLING TRAINING DOC_QUALITY TEAM_DISTRESS LAUNCH_GAP COMMS
  • ROUTINGConfusion about where escalated tickets go or who owns an issue type
  • TOOLINGTool or permissions blockers preventing specialists from doing their job
  • TRAININGSpecialists don't know how to handle a scenario or need enablement
  • DOC_QUALITYBeacon pages or internal docs that are missing, wrong, or outdated
  • TEAM_DISTRESSVolume spikes, queue pressure, complaints, or escalation surges
  • LAUNCH_GAPA shipped or upcoming feature is generating unexpected support burden with no readiness plan
  • COMMSCommunications, IHC articles, or enablement being shipped where specialists have not been briefed

Risk Levels

The overall daily risk level is assigned based on what the scan finds:

  • RED Active blocker affecting escalated specialists right now - tooling down, routing broken, or queue backed up with no path to clear. Needs immediate attention.
  • YELLOW 2+ signals in the same category, any LAUNCH_GAP signal, volume trending up, or a high-impact launch shipping within 7 days with no readiness plan. Worth monitoring.
  • GREEN Fewer than 3 signals total, none blocking, no imminent high-impact launches without readiness. Quiet day.

Priority Levels

Individual signals are also tagged with a priority:

P0 P1 P2 P3
  • P0 - CriticalImmediate action needed, hours to days. Blocking other work.
  • P1 - HighTime-sensitive, days to 2 weeks. Important operational impact.
  • P2 - Medium2-4 weeks. Moderate operational impact.
  • P3 - LowNice to have, deferrable. Minimal immediate impact.

Queue Health

The Queue Health tab shows a daily snapshot of Zendesk ticket queues for the two teams this dashboard covers. Data is pulled from BigQuery each morning alongside the signal check.

Queues Tracked

  • Technical Merchant Support (TMS)The primary async ticket queue for TMS specialists
  • SSA TeamThe ticket queue for Support Specialist Advisors

Retail TMS and Plus TMS are separate Zendesk groups and are not included in this view.

Metrics Shown

  • Active BacklogTickets in new, open, or pending status created within the last 30 days. Excludes on-hold tickets (waiting on merchant) and very old tickets.
  • UnassignedTickets with no assigned specialist - the "waiting to be picked up" count. This is the primary pressure indicator.
  • New (24h)Tickets created in the last 24 hours. Compared against the previous 24 hours to show volume trend (up or down arrow with percentage).
  • On HoldTickets on hold within the last 30 days - typically waiting on merchant response.
  • Age DistributionBreaks the active backlog into age buckets: 0-1 days (fresh), 2-3 days, 4-7 days, and 7-30 days. Helps spot when tickets are aging out.
  • Oldest Active TicketAge in days of the oldest non-hold ticket in the queue.

Queue Status Thresholds

  • RED Unassigned tickets exceed 150 OR active backlog exceeds 500. The queue is under fire and needs immediate support.
  • YELLOW Unassigned tickets exceed 50 OR active backlog exceeds 300. Pressure is building - worth monitoring closely.
  • GREEN Unassigned under 50 and active backlog under 300. Queue is healthy.

These thresholds are initial estimates and will be adjusted as we learn what normal looks like for each queue.
